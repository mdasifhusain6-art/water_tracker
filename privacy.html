1 Overview & Commitment

Welcome to Water Tracker ("Water Tracker", "we", "our", or "us"). We build smart, mindful habit-tracking tools to help you maintain optimal daily hydration and biological wellness.

This Privacy Policy explains how information about you is collected, used, disclosed, and safeguarded when you download and use the Water Tracker mobile application distributed via the Google Play Store.

Core Privacy Pledge: We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal health logs, weight, or contact information to data brokers, third-party advertisers, or marketing agencies.

3 How We Use Your Information

Your collected information is used solely for the following legitimate purposes:

  • Delivering Core Functionality: Recording water entries, computing percentage of daily goals, and displaying weekly/monthly progress trends.
  • Offline-First Resilience: Storing temporary intake logs locally on your device via Hive local database when no internet connection is present, and syncing them automatically when you reconnect.
  • Smart Reminders: Triggering periodic hydration notifications tailored to your waking hours while silencing alerts during your scheduled sleep period.
  • Security & Authentication: Validating your identity and safeguarding your cloud records using Supabase Auth.

4 Data Storage, Retention & Security

We implement rigorous security standards to ensure your hydration logs and personal data remain protected:

  • Encryption in Transit: All communications between the Water Tracker mobile app and our servers are encrypted using modern Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS).
  • Database Security: Cloud databases are powered by Supabase (PostgreSQL) hosted in high-security data centers with strict Row Level Security (RLS) policies ensuring users can only read and write their own data.
  • Local Storage Protection: Locally cached records (Hive boxes) remain stored inside the app's sandboxed local application directory on your mobile device.
  • Data Retention: We retain your water logs and profile settings for as long as your account remains active. If you delete your account, all associated data is permanently erased from active databases.

5 Third-Party Service Providers

We partner with select, reputable service providers who process data strictly under our instructions and in compliance with Google Play Developer Policies:

  • Supabase Inc.: Provides backend infrastructure, cloud authentication, and database services. Data is processed in accordance with Supabase's rigorous privacy and enterprise security protocols.
  • Google AdMob (Google LLC): We may display banner advertisements inside the app to support free maintenance and continuous development. AdMob may collect and process pseudonymous identifiers (e.g., Android Advertising ID) in accordance with Google's Privacy & Terms.

6 Android Device Permissions

Water Tracker requests only permissions that are strictly necessary for core features:

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Needed to sync hydration logs with Supabase cloud and load ads.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS (Android 13+): Required to deliver drink water reminders on your scheduled schedule. You can enable or disable this at any time in app Settings or your phone's notification settings.
  • SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM & RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED: Enables exact reminder delivery and ensures your daily water notification schedule is smoothly restored if your phone restarts.

7 Account & Data Deletion (Play Store Compliant)

Google Play requires apps with user accounts to provide an accessible path for account and data deletion. Water Tracker offers both in-app and web-based deletion pathways:

A

In-App Instant Deletion

Open Water Tracker → Go to Settings → Scroll down to ACCOUNT → Tap Delete Account & Data → Confirm deletion. Your profile and logs are immediately erased.

B

Web / Email Deletion Request

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot log in, email us at dhtech800@gmail.com with the subject "Account Deletion Request" from your registered email. We process requests within 7 business days.

Irreversible Action: Deletion removes your email, profile preferences, and all historical hydration intake records from our active databases permanently.

8 Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Water Tracker is not directed to individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under 13. If you become aware that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately, and we will promptly delete such records.

9 Your Global Privacy Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Depending on your geographic location, you enjoy certain legal rights under data privacy regulations:

  • Right to Access: You may inspect the personal information and hydration logs we maintain about you.
  • Right to Rectification: You may edit or correct inaccurate preferences, daily goals, or profile data directly within the app's Settings screen.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): You may request complete deletion of your records at any time.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: You may revoke notification or ad permissions via your mobile device system settings.
