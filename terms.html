2 Description of Service

Water Tracker is a mobile application developed to assist individuals in monitoring, recording, and visualizing their daily water intake. The Service features:

  • Interactive fluid intake tracking in milliliters (ml) and fluid ounces (oz).
  • Visual 3D holographic hydration progress models and daily target indicators.
  • Historical hydration logs with weekly bar charts and monthly trend analytics.
  • Periodic smart push notification reminders configured around user sleep schedules.
  • Offline data caching with automated cloud synchronization.

3 Health & Medical Disclaimer

Important Health Notice: Water Tracker is designed solely as a voluntary lifestyle and wellness habit-tracker. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, or clinical hydration prescriptions.

Every individual’s physical hydration needs vary depending on age, body weight, climate, physical activity, medications, pregnancy, and underlying medical conditions.

The default intake targets (such as 2,000 ml or 2.5 L) are general guidelines and should not replace advice given by qualified healthcare practitioners, physicians, or registered dietitians. Always consult a medical professional if you have kidney disorders, heart conditions, or are on fluid-restricted diets.
