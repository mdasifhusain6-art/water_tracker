Privacy Policy Summary

At Water Tracker, we take your personal privacy seriously. We collect only what is necessary to help you build healthy hydration habits and synchronize your logs safely.

Google Play Data Safety Certified: All data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS. We never sell your data to data brokers or third parties.

Key Highlights

  • What we store: Email address (for account), daily water intake logs (ml/oz), sleep/wake hours, optional weight.
  • How it's secured: Stored in encrypted Supabase PostgreSQL cloud with Row Level Security (RLS).
  • Offline support: Logs are cached locally on your device via Hive and synced automatically when connected.
  • Advertising: Supported by Google AdMob using standard privacy-respecting ad identifiers.
  • Your rights: You can view, export, or permanently delete your account & data anytime in-app or via web.

Account & Data Deletion Portal

In accordance with Google Play's Account Deletion Policy, Water Tracker provides two ways for users to permanently delete their account and all associated hydration data:

1

Method 1: Instant In-App Deletion

1. Open the Water Tracker app on your phone.
2. Navigate to Settings tab.
3. Scroll down to the ACCOUNT section.
4. Tap Delete Account & Data and confirm.
5. Your account, profile, and all water logs are immediately deleted.

2

Method 2: Web Deletion Request

If you uninstalled the app or lost device access, you can submit a deletion request via email directly below. We verify and permanently remove your records within 7 business days.

What data is deleted?
- Your authentication record (Email & user ID)
- Your user profile (Weight, daily goal, sleep/wake hours)
- All historical water entries, timestamps, and streak logs
This action is irreversible and cannot be recovered.
